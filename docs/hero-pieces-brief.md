# Hero flying pieces: image brief

These are the pieces that drift around the stallion and flip from front to back when clicked.
Every piece needs **two images: a front and a back**.

## Rules for every image

- **Style:** the same Art Nouveau look as the hero stallion: warm cream and gold, fine ink linework, marigold accents, flat lighting. No photo realism.
- **Background:** transparent. Export as PNG or WebP.
- **Canvas:** 1024 × 1024, with the piece centred and filling about 85% of the canvas.
- **Front and back must share one outline.** Same size, same shape and same position on the canvas, so the flip lines up edge to edge. Only the face design changes.
- **Straight on:** the piece faces the camera flat, with no tilt and no perspective. The flip adds the 3D.
- **No drop shadow** baked into the image. The site adds its own.
- **No real brand logos, and no text except what is listed below.**

## Where to put them

Drop all 16 files into a single folder and share it, or put them straight into
`stallion-marathon/public/hero/pieces/`. If some pieces are not ready, start with a few
(for example coin, medal and card) and the rest can follow.
